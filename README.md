# Purix Raw — one-knob spectral cleanup

![Purix Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/PurixRaw/main/purixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob to tame harsh resonances, boxiness, and sibilance.**

Purix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Purix — turn a single PURIFY knob and its spectral suppressor tracks and pulls down the harshest peaks in real time, auto-leveled so the sound gets cleaner, not quieter. Works on vocals, drums, synths, and full mixes.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/PurixRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`PurixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **PURIFY** knob. Turn it up and Purix's spectral suppressor tracks the peaks in your signal and tames harsh resonances, boxiness, and sibilance in real time, with auto-leveling so the output stays consistent as you push it.

That's the whole interface — set it and listen. Because it works on the frequency spectrum, Purix Raw reports a small amount of latency to your DAW (automatically compensated by the host), so use it while mixing rather than for live monitoring.
